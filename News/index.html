Windows Secure Boot: BlackX bootkit bypasses Secure Boot on patched systems

The BlackX UEFI bootkit uses the Baton Drop flaw to bypass Secure Boot on Windows systems that are patched but do not have the related revocations. It has been used in the wild against diplomatic organizations in Central Asia. Patching alone does not protect a system; the Secure Boot revocations must also be applied.
