Microsoft 365: Fake outage notices claim held messages will be deleted

Microsoft Threat Intelligence saw a high-volume campaign on November 13 that impersonated Microsoft 365 service-health notices. The emails claimed messages were "held" during the outage and would be deleted within 24 hours, and their "RESTORE MESSAGES"-style buttons led to a non-Microsoft site. Subjects follow the pattern "Outage – <token>" and sender domains rotate. Microsoft recommends Safe Links/ZAP, phishing-resistant MFA and revoking sessions for anyone who clicked.

All users: GTA 6 launch lures, including fake "free pre-order" employee perks

KnowBe4 reports phishing that impersonates Rockstar Games ahead of the November 19 Grand Theft Auto 6 launch. Targeted lures pose as employee perks ("free GTA 6 pre-order") and use QR codes to move victims onto their personal phones. GTA-themed lures made up 9.6% of phishing volume in the week of October 26.

All users: "Password expiring today" email leads to a convincing branded login page

A "password expiring today" email linked to a credential-harvesting page hosted on Google Cloud Storage. The page showed a login form tailored to the victim's domain, laid over a screenshot of the organization's own website. It sent the password, IP address, location, browser details and the domain's MX records to the attacker through Telegram.

iPhone + iPad: Mic-permission bypass detailed; fixed in iOS 26.6.1 and 18.7.11

Researchers published details of CVE-2026-65339, a flaw in Apple's audio service that let apps record audio even after the user denied microphone access. Apple fixed it in iOS/iPadOS 26.6.1 (August 17) and 18.7.11 (October 26), and the fix is also in iOS 27. There is no report of in-the-wild exploitation, and CISA scores it CVSS 5.0.

Windows: Unpatched "SignDrop" CLFS exploit gives standard users SYSTEM

A public proof-of-concept abuses the CLFS driver together with the Defender antimalware service to escalate from a standard user to SYSTEM. It was tested on Windows 11 26H2 and Windows Server 2025 Experimental. There is no CVE or patch yet and no reported in-the-wild exploitation, one independent tester found the exploit unreliable, and Microsoft is investigating.

Windows 11: Remote Lock for lost or stolen devices enters testing

Remote Lock lets IT admins lock a lost, stolen or compromised Windows device without wiping it. It signs out all sessions and shows a "Device Locked" screen with a reason and helpdesk contact. The device stays enrolled with BitLocker active, and only an admin can unlock it. It is in testing in the Windows Insider Experimental channel (build 29708.100x).

Apple Mac: macOS 27.3 beta tightens app and Full Disk Access permissions

macOS 27.3 beta 2 splits the "Device Control and Data Access" permission into "System & App Protection Override" and "Accessibility", and tightens Full Disk Access. Users can limit access to specific apps or folders, set "ask every time", and see an indicator when one app controls another. Existing grants are kept, and no release date has been given.

Microsoft 365: Bug introduced in July caused last week's global outage

Microsoft attributed the November 12 worldwide Microsoft 365 outage to a software defect added in a July update to network gateway servers. The bug was triggered by traffic shifts during network maintenance and affected Exchange Online, Outlook and Teams. Microsoft has promised a full post-incident report.

Barracuda: Cloud breach began with a vendor access key that was never revoked

An attacker used a cloud access key Barracuda had issued to a third-party AI vendor and never retired. Over about 44 hours it moved through roughly 10 cloud accounts and Kubernetes clusters, using tooling that matches the ARTEX framework. Barracuda asked affected Managed XDR Cloud and Monitored Endpoint customers to recreate their API credentials. Email Security products were not named in the disclosure.
