Entra ID: Attacker uses stolen app secrets to enter MSP tenants and clients

Todyl reports that since Nov 1 a single operator has used stolen but still-valid Entra app client secrets, an Azure DevOps federated identity and replayed refresh tokens to sign into MSP Microsoft 365 and Azure tenants, reaching 27 organizations including downstream clients. No Microsoft vulnerability was involved and the activity is ongoing; about 2,279 mailbox items were accessed and 81 files downloaded. Recommended steps include blocking IP 199.19.77.84 with a Conditional Access policy for workload identities, removing suspicious secrets and federated credentials, revoking sessions and PATs, and rotating Key Vault secrets.

WordPress: Core RCE flaw exploited in the wild, added to CISA KEV

CISA added CVE-2026-119977 (CVSS 8.1) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a federal patch deadline of November 6, 2026. The race condition in full-package core upgrades lets unauthenticated attackers run code during the upgrade window; Wordfence blocked 12,632 attempts in 24 hours. Patches were released in late October (GHSA-4w3m-c66w-69mg), but the sources do not name the fixed version.

Apple devices: APT29 zero-click campaign exploits flaws in older iOS and macOS

CISA added Apple PAC bypass CVE-2024-27834 to KEV on November 4. Sekoia ties it to a presumed APT29 watering-hole campaign that delivers cookie-stealing malware through hijacked sites, fake domains and a compromised analytics script. The exploit chains target older releases (iOS 13 to 17.2.1 and iOS 18.4 to 18.7). Fixes exist in iOS/iPadOS 16.7.8 and 17.5, macOS Sonoma 14.5 and Safari 17.5, and current iOS 27.x is not listed as a target.

Barracuda: Two chainable ESG flaws can give attackers root access

CVE-2026-116606 (CVSS 9.3) is a pre-authentication authorization bypass in Email Security Gateway, and CVE-2026-111560 (CVSS 8.6) is a command injection flaw affecting ESG and several other Barracuda appliances. Chained, they could give root access, but exploitation requires access to the admin interface and no attacks are reported. Devices several versions behind may need more than one update.

Apple iCloud: Publicly shared iCloud links scanned for harmful content from 27.3

Starting with iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS 27.3, expected in December, Apple will check photos and files shared through iCloud "Anyone with the Link" for harmful content.
