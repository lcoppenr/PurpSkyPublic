Barracuda ESG: Two exploited Email Security Gateway flaws chain to root access

Singapore's CSA warns that CVE-2026-116606 (CVSS 9.8) and CVE-2026-111560 in Barracuda Email Security Gateway are being exploited, and public proof-of-concept code exists; chained together they give an attacker root on the device. Barracuda confirmed limited exploitation on November 9, and CISA added both flaws to its KEV catalog. Patches are available, and CVE-2026-111560 affects four products that each need a separate patch.

Windows laptops: Physical flash-chip attack weakens Windows Hello and BitLocker PINs

Researchers at TU Berlin found that on AMD-based PCs and some Intel PCs, someone who opens the case can reset the TPM lockout for Windows Hello and BitLocker PINs. That makes brute-forcing a PIN take days instead of years. The attack needs physical access, and the report does not mention a fix.
