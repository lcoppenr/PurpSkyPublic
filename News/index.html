Payroll Pirates AitM campaign hijacks M365 accounts to steal payroll and finance emails, bypasses MFA Security Act Today Score: 11/11

Arctic Wolf Labs and Microsoft (tracking as Storm-2755) report an active widespread AitM phishing campaign using voicemail-themed lures and residential proxies to hijack Microsoft 365 sessions post-MFA. Attackers target payroll and finance personnel and redirect salary payments to attacker-controlled accounts. Hundreds of organizations across US, Canada, and Europe (healthcare, education, manufacturing, government, professional services) have been hit -- every compromised account had MFA enabled, which is why phish-resistant methods (FIDO2 / passkeys / certificate-based) matter more than ever for M365 Business tenants.
