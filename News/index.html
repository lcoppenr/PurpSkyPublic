Apple devices: CoreGraphics zero-day exploited in attacks, now on CISA KEV

Apple fixed CVE-2026-86950, an out-of-bounds write in CoreGraphics where a crafted file can lead to arbitrary code execution. Apple says it may have been exploited in an "extremely sophisticated" attack against targeted individuals, and CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 29 with an October 2 federal deadline. Fixes are in iOS/iPadOS 26.7.1, macOS Tahoe 26.7.1 and macOS Sequoia 15.8.1, and the current iOS 27 / macOS 27 branch already includes the fix.

Attackers promote malicious custom ChatGPT GPTs through sponsored Google search results. The links go to sites that use ClickFix social engineering to install a multi-stage remote access trojan. The campaign is active, and no patch applies because it relies on tricking users.
