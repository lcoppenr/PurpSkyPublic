Apple devices: Old patched Apple flaws exploited in the wild, added to CISA KEV

CISA added CVE-2024-27834, a Pointer Authentication bypass, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 4, 2026, after exploitation linked to APT29 to deliver info-stealing malware. Apple fixed it in May 2024 (iOS/iPadOS 16.7.8 and 17.5, macOS Sonoma 14.5, Safari 17.5), so only unpatched devices are at risk. A related APT29 watering-hole campaign uses compromised sites and a hijacked analytics script to exploit previously patched Safari/WebKit flaws, and fully patched iPhones and iPads are not exposed to the known chain.
