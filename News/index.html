SonicWall TZ: Exploited SSL-VPN flaw lets attackers bypass SAML MFA

CVE-2026-19868 lets an attacker who holds valid credentials for another sign-in method get past SAML controls on SSL-VPN, possibly including MFA enforced through SAML. CISA has added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and SonicWall says there is no workaround. The same update fixes six other SonicOS flaws: two management-interface flaws rated 8.8, plus pre-authentication denial-of-service bugs in SSL-VPN and TACACS+. Affected versions are Gen7 7.3.3-7015 and earlier; the fixed versions are Gen7 7.3.4-7013 and Gen8 8.2.3-8015.

WordPress: Exploited core flaw fixed in 7.1.4, but updating carries risk

CVE-2026-119977 affects WordPress 4.7 through 7.1.3. It is in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and Wordfence saw attack attempts within minutes of the fix's release. On 5.9 and later the update itself unpacks into a predictably named wp-content/upgrade folder, where an unauthenticated attacker could run PHP. WP-CLI uses the same updater and is not a safe alternative; versions 4.6 and earlier get no fix.
