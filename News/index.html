SonicWall TZ: Exploited SSL-VPN SAML MFA bypass fixed in new SonicOS

SonicWall fixed seven SonicOS flaws, including CVE-2026-19868, an SSL-VPN SAML authentication bypass that can get around SAML-enforced MFA. No workaround exists, and CISA added it to the KEV catalog with a November 20, 2026 deadline for federal agencies. Affected versions are Gen7 7.3.3-7015 and earlier and Gen8 8.2.2-8015 and earlier, and the article does not say which hardware models (such as TZ) are affected.

WordPress: Exploited core flaw CVE-2026-119977 fixed in 7.1.4

CVE-2026-119977 affects WordPress core 4.7 through 7.1.3. It is actively exploited and listed in CISA KEV, and fixes exist for older branches back to 4.7, while 4.6 and earlier get no fix. A researcher found that sites can still be attacked while the fix itself is installing.
