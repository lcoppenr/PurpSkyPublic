iPhone, iPad, Mac: CoreGraphics zero-day exploited, public exploit code now out

CVE-2026-86950, a CoreGraphics flaw affecting iOS, iPadOS and macOS, is exploited in the wild, and CISA has added it to its KEV catalog. The first public proof-of-concept has now been released, and researchers point to crafted PDFs as a possible delivery path. Fixes are in iOS/iPadOS 26.7.1, macOS Tahoe 26.7.1, macOS Sequoia 15.8.1 and the iOS/macOS 27 releases.

Exchange Online: EWS retirement starts today; apps that use it will stop working

Microsoft starts retiring Exchange Web Services in Exchange Online on October 1, 2026, and apps that still use EWS will stop working. Microsoft says to move them to Microsoft Graph, Power Platform or Copilot declarative agents. Outlook for Windows and Mac and Teams are not affected.

All users: Fake ChatGPT GPTs lead to "paste this command" malware tricks

Attackers are building malicious Custom GPTs disguised as legitimate products. These send users to sites with ClickFix lures that trick them into running commands, which install remote access trojans. Huntress saw the activity in late September 2026.

Executives: Phishing aimed at US executives steals Microsoft 365 sessions

A phishing campaign aimed at US executives steals Microsoft 365 session tokens and then installs RMM tools for persistent access. Because the session itself is stolen, MFA does not stop it. Token protection and Conditional Access session controls are the relevant defenses.

Email phishing: Russian group uses fake event invites to deliver a backdoor

Star Blizzard, a Russian state-backed group, uses a delivery chain called RedFlick. It starts with harmless-looking professional email exchanges and fake event invitations, then delivers the CosmicPulse backdoor. Reports count at least 13 campaigns from January to August 2026 reaching more than 100 organizations, mostly in the US and UK.
