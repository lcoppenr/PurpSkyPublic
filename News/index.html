Details

Mac users: Fake Zoom installer drops password-stealing backdoor on macOS

Jamf Threat Labs describes CloudSyncD, a macOS backdoor spread through a fake Zoom installer. It phishes the user's password and hides it using invisible zero-width Unicode characters. This is malware rather than a product flaw, so there is no patch; the defense is installing Zoom only from official sources.

Microsoft: October 2026 security updates

Microsoft published CVE-2026-96940, a weak-authorization flaw in Exchange Server that lets an authenticated attacker elevate privileges over the network. The source does not say whether it is exploited in the wild. Affected builds and the security update are listed in the MSRC entry.

Microsoft Edge: Chromium V8 and ANGLE flaws fixed in Edge

MSRC listed three Chromium flaws that affect Edge: CVE-2025-1920 (V8 type confusion), CVE-2025-2137 (V8 out-of-bounds read) and CVE-2025-10502 (ANGLE heap buffer overflow). The sources do not say whether any of them are exploited. The fixes ship through Chromium/Edge browser updates.

A new Microsoft 365 Roadmap item adds Impersonation Protection to Teams meetings. It helps users spot participants and meeting organizers who may be deceptive. It is aimed at social-engineering attacks carried out through Teams meetings.
