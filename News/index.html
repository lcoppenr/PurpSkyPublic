Synology DSM: C3RB3R ransomware actively exploiting outdated NAS devices

Synology says C3RB3R (Cerber-family) ransomware attacks are increasing, with attackers exploiting a vulnerability to encrypt files and rename them with a .L0CK3D extension. Synology points to advisory Synology-SA-24:20 (CVE-2024-10441, CVSS 9.8 RCE, plus CVE-2024-50629 and CVE-2024-10445), but a secondary report blames CVE-2026-13639 and CVE-2026-13684, fixed in Synology-SA-26:13 (September 18, 2026). Synology recommends updating DSM and packages to the current release.

WordPress: Unpatched CVSS 10.0 CryptoPayment Gateway plugin flaw under attack

CVE-2026-81648 (CVSS 10.0) in the CryptoPayment Gateway plugin, versions 1.2.1 through 1.2.2 (the latest), can expose wallet credentials, and Defused Cyber has recorded exploitation attempts in the wild. There is no fix, and the plugin was removed from the WordPress Plugin Directory on August 28, 2026.
