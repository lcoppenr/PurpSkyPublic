SonicWall SonicOS access-control bypass (CVE-2026-0204) impacts Gen 7 TZ firewalls Security Act Today Score: 10/11

SonicWall disclosed CVE-2026-0204, a CVSS 8.0 improper access control flaw in SonicOS across Gen 6, Gen 7, and Gen 8 firewalls, including the TZ 270-670 series. Affected versions include SonicOS 7.3.1-7013 and prior. PSIRT strongly recommends disabling HTTP/HTTPS management and SSLVPN on all interfaces until patched, restricting management to SSH.
